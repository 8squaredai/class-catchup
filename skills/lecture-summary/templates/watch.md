# Template: Watch guide 🧭

For a long recording the student should partly rewatch. Map the session so they can jump
straight to what matters.

## `markdown` (plus add-ons)

```
# <Course code>: <lecture title> (<date>)

**In one sentence:** <what this session was about>

## Chapter map
- **[mm:ss–mm:ss] <Chapter title>** ⭐ Must-watch: <one sentence on what happens and why it matters>
- **[mm:ss–mm:ss] <Chapter title>** ▶️ Worth a look: ...
- **[mm:ss–mm:ss] <Chapter title>** ⏩ Skippable: <why it is safe to skip>

## If you only have 15 minutes
- [mm:ss–mm:ss] <Chapter title> (~N min)

## Announcements
- ... [mm:ss]
```

- Chapter map: 5–12 chapters covering the whole recording in order, no gaps. A chapter starts at
  the timestamp of its first line and ends where the next chapter starts. Both ends must be
  timestamps from the transcript.
- Rate every chapter: ⭐ Must-watch (new core content or anything graded/announced),
  ▶️ Worth a look (examples, discussion), ⏩ Skippable (housekeeping, repetition, off-topic).
- "If you only have 15 minutes": the must-watch chapters, with approximate minutes from the
  timestamps; if they add up to more than 15 minutes, pick the most important ones.
- Omit "Announcements" if there were none.

## `whatsapp_text` (under 1,200 characters)

```
🧭 *<Course code> – <lecture title>* (<date>)
How to catch up on the recording:

⭐ *Must-watch*
• [mm:ss–mm:ss] <Chapter title>
⏩ *Safe to skip*
• [mm:ss–mm:ss] <Chapter title>

⏱ The must-watch parts are about N minutes in total.

💬 Reply with any question about this class and I'll answer from the recording.
```
