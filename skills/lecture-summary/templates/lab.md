# Template: Lab / workshop 🧪

For hands-on, studio or discussion sessions, where the value was in doing, not listening.

## `markdown` (roughly 250–500 words, plus add-ons)

```
# <Course code>: <lecture title> (<date>)

**In one sentence:** <what the session was about>

## The activity
- <what students did, the rules or constraints, groups/roles, materials> [mm:ss]

## What came out of it
- <findings or ideas groups shared, and the professor's feedback or debrief> [mm:ss]

## How to catch up
1. <concrete step for the absent student> [mm:ss]

## Deadlines
- ... [mm:ss]
```

- What came out of it: describe groups as "one group", "another group"; never name students.
- How to catch up: numbered, concrete steps based on what the professor said (join a team, do the
  exercise alone, submit something). If the professor gave no instructions for absent students,
  write: "The professor did not say how to make up this session; ask them."
- Omit "Deadlines" if there were none.
